# Alchemy Table

The Alchemy Table is a table for making mash more effectively!

This table lets you slot a mortar and pestle into the left side, 
freeing up the crafting slot it normally reserves so you can make mash with up to 9 ingredients!

In addition, the Alchemy Table makes use of the Alchemindex used in its construction,
allowing you to filter the items in your inventory by known effects.
This will gray out any slots for items you don't already know contain that effect.
It can even be used to search for only items which have at least 1 undiscovered effect.
Just click the effect to the left of the interface, or click the red "X" to clear your filter.
